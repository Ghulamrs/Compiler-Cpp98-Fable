GAP ANALYSIS OF C++98

Left for later sessions, all recorded in docs/next-targets.md with repros:

Polymorphic virtual bases (14b): the largest remaining item. They are refused today, not miscompiled.
Cases that still behave differently from cl:
takeV(mk()) copies twice and destroys in a different order;
->* reads garbage;
the -O2 double-to-unsigned conversion;
ill-formed hierarchies cl rejects are accepted.
Mixed cl/cppparse links only: a halfway float literal; statics in local classes; vtable names with longer base paths; exceptions crossing runtimes.
Code size: the plan to bring it from about 1.85 down to about 1.25 of cl.
The behaviour gaps are the ones to tackle first next time, as they are the remaining silent-difference cases.
